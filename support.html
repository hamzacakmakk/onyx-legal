Support

Write to the support email listed on the app's App Store page. Tell us the device and OS version and, if it is about a specific document, how many pages it has — that is usually enough to reproduce anything.

I paid for Pro but the app still shows the paywall

Open Settings → Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you bought with. Purchases are tied to that store account, not to an Onyx account — there are none.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Onyx Scan. On Android: Google Play → your profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel anything.

The lifetime purchase is a single payment and has nothing to cancel.

Text recognition found nothing

On-device OCR needs reasonably sharp, reasonably level text. Rescan with more light, get the page filling more of the frame, and try the B & W filter for dense text. Handwriting is recognised much less reliably than print.

The exported PDF is not searchable

Run text recognition on the document first, and check that Settings → Searchable PDF is on. The text layer is built from what OCR recognised, so a document that recognised nothing exports as a normal, valid PDF without one.

I lost my scans

Onyx has no servers and keeps no copy, so we cannot restore anything. Scans live only on the device, which is also why they are private. Export what matters to you.

I forgot the password on an exported PDF

That password is applied to the exported file itself and is not stored anywhere — not in the app, and not by us. It cannot be recovered. Export the document again from the app with a new password.

Face ID will not unlock a document

The lock uses the device's own biometric check, so it also accepts your device passcode as a fallback. If biometrics are unavailable, the passcode is the way in.
