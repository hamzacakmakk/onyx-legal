Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

Onyx Scan has no servers. Your documents, scans, recognised text and signatures are stored on your device and are never uploaded to us, because there is nowhere for them to be uploaded to.

Who we are

Onyx Scan is a document scanner for iOS and Android. This policy covers the app and this website. In this policy, “we” means the developer of Onyx Scan and “you” means the person using it.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in the app:

  • the scans themselves, and the original captures behind them;
  • document titles, folders and the order of pages;
  • text recognised by the on-device OCR, and the invisible text layer in exported PDFs;
  • signatures you draw and notes you type;
  • your settings, including the language, appearance and export preferences.

Scanning, edge detection, filters and text recognition all run on your device using the operating system's own frameworks (VisionKit on iOS, ML Kit on Android). They work in airplane mode, which is the simplest proof that nothing is being sent anywhere.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no backup on our side, and we cannot recover anything for you.

What we collect

We do not collect, store or have access to your documents, your scanned text, your files or any account information — there are no accounts. We do not operate analytics of our own.

Third parties inside the app

Two services run inside the app. Neither one ever receives your documents, your scans or the text inside them.

Service What it does and what it receives
Google AdMob
free tier only		 Shows advertising to users on the free tier. To do so, Google may process device and usage data such as an advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, device model and ad interactions. Read how Google uses information from apps that use its services. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we show Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and you can change that choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. On iOS you are also asked separately, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, before any tracking identifier is used. Onyx Pro removes advertising entirely.
RevenueCat
purchases only		 Manages subscriptions and the one-off purchase, and checks whether you are entitled to Pro. It receives a randomly generated app user identifier and the purchase receipt issued by Apple or Google. It does not receive your name, your email address or anything you scanned. Read RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Payments themselves are handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your card details.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera — to scan a document. The camera is used only while you are scanning, and the image goes straight into the app's own storage.
  • Photo library — only if you choose to turn an existing photo into a PDF. The app reads the photos you pick and nothing else.
  • Face ID / biometrics — only to unlock documents you have locked. The check happens on the device; we never receive biometric data, and neither does the app.
  • Tracking (iOS) — asked once, and only in relation to advertising. Declining is fine: ads simply become non-personalised.

What leaves your device, and only when you ask

When you tap share, print or export, the finished PDF or text file is handed to the operating system's own share sheet, and from there it goes wherever you send it — mail, a messaging app, a cloud drive, a printer. That destination is your choice and its own privacy policy applies. We are not part of that transfer and we do not keep a copy.

Children

Onyx Scan is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect anything from them. Advertising is requested with a content rating of PG.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on request. The data is on your device and entirely under your control: you can delete any document from inside the app, and deleting the app removes everything at once. Where they apply, your rights under the GDPR, the UK GDPR and the CCPA are unaffected by this — you can still exercise them against the third parties listed above, directly with them.

We do not sell or share personal information, as those terms are defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes with it and the new version is published here before the change reaches the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about anything the app does with your data: the support email listed on the app's App Store page.
