A document scanner that never uploads anything.

Point the camera at a page. Onyx finds the edges, straightens the perspective and makes a PDF — entirely on your phone. No account, no cloud, no watermark, and exporting a PDF is free forever.

Scanning, filters and text recognition all run on the device. The app works in airplane mode, which is the simplest proof that nothing is being sent anywhere.

What it does

  • Edge detection and perspective correction from the OS scanner, with batch capture
  • Five filters plus a per-page brightness and contrast trim
  • Re-crop against the untouched original, rotate, reorder, delete, extract
  • Merge, split and duplicate documents
  • Draw a signature once and stamp it anywhere; typed notes and date stamps
  • On-device text recognition, and a searchable PDF with a real text layer
  • Print, share the recognised text, or export a password-protected PDF
  • Folders, search inside scans, multi-select, and Face ID document locking

Free, always

Unlimited scans and pages, no watermark, PDF export, the searchable text layer, cropping and signatures. Onyx Pro adds unlimited text recognition, search inside every scan, unlimited folders, document locking, password-protected export and custom export settings — and removes advertising.
